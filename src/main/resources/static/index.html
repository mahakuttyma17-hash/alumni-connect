About the platform

A single home for your alumni community

Alumni Connect replaces scattered spreadsheets and group chats with one secure portal. Alumni keep their own profiles up to date, administrators manage records responsibly, and everyone can see how the community is growing.

  • Create a personal alumni profile and choose whether it appears in the directory.
  • Search peers by name, department, batch, company or job title.
  • Record donations, volunteering and other contributions.
  • Administrators manage alumni records and review contributions.
